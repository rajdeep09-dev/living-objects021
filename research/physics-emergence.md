# Research Note: Physics Emergence in BEAST v4

The v4 physics engine operationalizes “emergent physics” as a population of named, composable transformations whose mutations are accepted only with a formal safety witness. A universe branch is a durable lineage edge plus a changed law fingerprint. Divergence is therefore measurable in code space.

The most useful next experiment is selection pressure over law bundles: compare populations that optimize token conservation, information throughput, or behavioral diversity, while keeping a fixed invariant checker. The result should be reported as regime diversity, stability, and rollback cost—not as evidence that an artificial universe is physically real.
