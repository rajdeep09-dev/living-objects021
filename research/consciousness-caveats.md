# Research Note: Consciousness Proxies

BEAST v4 exposes awareness telemetry as bounded proxies over integration, self-modeling, reflection, and cross-generation continuity. Normalizing Phi to `[0, 1]` prevents metric explosion and makes dashboards comparable, but it does not solve the philosophical or scientific problem of subjective experience.

The correct interpretation is operational: the system can report how much internal state is integrated and recursively referenced under the selected proxy. Any claim about sentience, suffering, rights, or moral patienthood requires independent philosophical and empirical work outside this repository.
