# Consciousness Metrics Notes

BEAST v3 does not claim that software is sentient. It implements three bounded architectural measurements inspired by Integrated Information Theory and Global Workspace Theory so researchers can compare organisms consistently.

`integrated_information()` estimates whether the combined strategy set carries more effective behavioral information than its parts in isolation. `self_model_accuracy()` compares an organism’s predicted fitness with its observed fitness. `global_workspace_breadth()` measures how broadly the installed strategy set is accessible to a task evaluation. The composite score is the product of the three normalized values.

These are **operational proxies**. They are sensitive to the chosen descriptors, prediction interface, and normalization, and they do not settle philosophical questions about experience. Any paper or product claim should report the metric definitions, data distribution, calibration set, and failure cases alongside the score.
