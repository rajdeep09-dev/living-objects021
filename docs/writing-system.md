# Emergent Writing Systems

`WritingSystem` maps strategy intents into a compact token stream, grows vocabulary lazily, preserves grammar layers, and translates known tokens through a shared meaning table. It demonstrates externalized strategy semantics, not a claim that the system has produced a human language.

Track vocabulary growth, grammar depth, round-trip fidelity, translation loss, and cultural retention after the originating organism is removed. Compare mutation pressure and population size against random-symbol baselines.
